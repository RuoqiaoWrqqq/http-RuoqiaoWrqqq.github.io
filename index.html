Research 🧬

AI × Immunology × Single-cell

Turning noisy immune data into testable biological stories.

I build models that connect cell states, pathway activity, regulatory logic, and disease-specific perturbations. The fun part: making large-scale omics data say something mechanistic, not just colorful.

🧫

Immune Digital Twins

Virtual patient simulation and in silico perturbation frameworks for coordinated monocyte immune programs.

 🕸️

Gene Regulatory Networks

Condition-specific regulatory rewiring using prior knowledge, foundation models, and graph attention networks.

 📈

Single-Cell Pathway Scoring

Benchmarking and method development for pathway activity scoring at single-cell resolution.

 🧩

Multi-omics Integration

scRNA-seq, CITE-seq, BCR-seq, scATAC-seq, spatial transcriptomics, and proteomics.
